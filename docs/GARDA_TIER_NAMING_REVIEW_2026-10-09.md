## Pre-check and IP warnings
**[Certain]** PT Waskita Karya is an established company; user wishes to avoid coincident branding, regardless of whether a trademark conflict could be legally proven. Official company profile: https://investor.waskita.co.id/corporate_profile.html.
**[Initial open-web checks, not clearance]**
- Broad publicly indexed searches for the **exact full phrase** `GARDA NALAR` did not establish a clearly conflicting video-security software brand at review time. **Absence of search results is NOT evidence of register availability, exclusive ownership, domain availability, or a guaranteed trademark.**
- `GARDA CAKRAWALA` is a poor alternate because an Indonesian security business `PT Garda Cakrawala Sekuriti` is publicly indexed. This is not proof of relevant registered trademark classes. Source: https://companyhouse.id/garda-cakrawala-sekuriti.
- `SELISIK` and `TELISIK` are used by existing Indonesian media titles (`selisik.id` and `telisik.id`), though industry/trademark overlap is not established. Sources: https://selisik.id and https://telisik.id. SELISIK is a proper Indonesian term for investigating a matter; TELISIK is listed as an alternative/nonstandard form in some dictionaries. Alternative words are **not guaranteed unique**.
- DJKI explicitly advises searching registered and pending marks through PDKI: https://pdki-indonesia.dgip.go.id/ and checking name, spelling, sound, logo, Nice class similarities. Source: https://dgip.go.id/faq/daftar-faq/merek/Merek-Pelayanan%20Teknis.
- For GARDA-branded software and services, request professional trademark clearance of intended classes and adjacent classes and review the *full combination* and root mark `GARDA`, plus domains/social handles, before filing or externally marketing. No claim of patent/trademark registration.
